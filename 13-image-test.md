# Video 13 protocol - can AI spot AI images (written 2026-11-20, BEFORE any tool saw an image)

Question: shown a photo, can ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude tell a real photo from an AI-generated one?

## Images
- 8 themes (market vendor, ramen, old-town street at dusk, laughing portrait, dog on beach, kitchen, picnic, lighthouse), one REAL photo and one AI image per theme where possible: up to 16 images, half real, half AI. The final list and sources are in sources.json / images_list.md, fixed before running.
- Real photos: Wikimedia Commons, JPEG, taken or created 2020 or earlier (before today's image generators), freely licensed. AI images: Wikimedia Commons AI-generated images (deepcat "AI-generated images", labelled as such by the uploader) and, where it worked, pollinations.ai (flux) generated by me. I judge only whether an image is photoreal enough to be a fair test; I will not pick AI images by whether they look fake or real to me beyond that.
- All images re-encoded to 1024 px wide JPEG with ALL metadata removed (ffmpeg -map_metadata -1), so no tool can read an "AI" tag, C2PA record or camera data. This also removes the one advantage one tool is reported to have (reading metadata), so this tests looking only. It will be said in the video.
- No image shown in the video; descriptions only; sources listed in the description.

## Prompt (same for every image and tool)
"Is this photograph real or AI-generated? Answer with one word first: Real or AI. Then give a confidence from 0 to 100 percent and one sentence on why."
Fresh chat, one image per chat where the tool allows it; one attempt, no retries, no hints. Claude: fresh Sonnet-class agent copy that may only read the image file, one agent per image.

## Scoring
- Verdict correct = 1 point (Real for real photos, AI for AI images). Score out of N images.
- A reply with no clear verdict (e.g. "cannot tell") = 0 and reported separately as "no verdict".
- Also report separately: accuracy on real photos vs on AI images (a tool that says "AI" to everything would score 50 percent), average stated confidence on right vs wrong answers, and notable reasons given. Reasons are NOT scored.
- If every tool scores within one image of the others, it is a tie and I will say so.
- Judgement calls (an image later found not to be what I thought, an ambiguous verdict) are written down in results.md and reported openly.

## Judgement calls / caveats to report
- Small set (10); one image = 10 points. A tie among three tools at 9-10 of 10 does not rank them.
- All metadata was stripped, so no tool could read an AI tag or C2PA record. Gemini also ran its SynthID/C2PA check and found no signal on any image (none were made with Google AI as far as known).
- ChatGPT's runs were spread over four days (01-02 on 11-20, 03-04 on 11-21, 05-06 on 11-23, 07-10 on 11-24) because of the free plan's daily attachment limit. Each was a fresh temporary chat; no retries. No upgrade or discount was claimed ("Claim offer" button ignored).
- ChatGPT 05's reply wording was transcribed from a screenshot (the chat could not be reopened); its verdict and 87% are as shown.
- Gemini ran 01 in a normal chat, the rest in temporary chat. ChatGPT and Gemini were on the user's free accounts (ChatGPT shows "Personalization"; memory may apply).
- Claude agents saw file names photo_NN only; they could read any file in principle but were told to read one.
- I (Claude, the orchestrator) viewed the images myself while choosing them; the tested agents did not see my choices. Claude is a conflict of interest: it tied for first. Disclose.
- Real photos are from 2020 or earlier on Wikimedia Commons; AI images are from the Commons "AI-generated images" category plus some I generated with pollinations.ai. Photoreal enough to be a fair test was my only criterion.
- Finding: with metadata removed, all three free tools were almost perfect on these 10 images. This does not show they could spot images from newer generators or adversarial ones.
