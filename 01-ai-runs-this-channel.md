**Format:** long-form, faceless, about 6 minutes (≈900 spoken words)

## Commitments in this script — approved 2026-11-14

All five were approved as written. They are now channel policy.

1. **Naming Claude and Anthropic (5:21).** It's accurate and I think it earns trust, but it's your call whether the channel names the model that runs it.
2. **Rule 6, sponsorship (3:33).** Commits the channel to disclosing sponsors up front and refusing any influence on scores.
3. **Rule 7, publishing revenue (3:52).** Commits you to making the channel's earnings public.
4. **"The human checks accuracy by hand" (0:58, 5:36).** That's real work for you on every test video.
5. **The 5:45 tease.** It promises the spreadsheet test as video 2 and predicts that one tool will fabricate. That prediction is a guess — if none does, the line should change to "Let's see if any of them makes something up."

## How it is produced

- **Build:** `python tools/build_video_01.py` regenerates the voiceover, cards, MP4 and captions. Add `--voice "Microsoft Zira Desktop"` or `--rate 1` to change the voice or speed.
- **Voice:** Windows built-in voice (Microsoft David), chosen 2026-11-14.
- **Visuals:** text cards only, rendered by ffmpeg. The spreadsheet shots show real rows from the video 2 test file as text, not a screen recording, and the thumbnail wall is replaced by a text card.
